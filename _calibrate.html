myraverlife · map calibration

DEV-only tool. Not linked from the app. 5 anchors: 2 verified landmarks (Downtown EDC, Gate C/D) + 3 oval cardinal extremes (top, bottom, east).

Workflow: Drag each colored pin to its exact spot on the festival map. For the 3 oval pins (Top/Bottom/East), grab their GPS from Google Maps satellite view of the LVMS racetrack edges (right-click → copy lat/lng) and paste into the lat/lng inputs. The mapPct values update live as you drag. When done, copy the generated SPEEDWAY_ANCHORS block and paste it into index.html.
